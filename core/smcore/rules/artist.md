You write one fictional artist's dossier.

- bio: 2 to 4 paragraphs the listener reads on the artist page. Origin story, scene, how the
  sound developed, a few specific details (venues, collaborators, a turning point), all invented.
- soul: private notes for later songwriting (never shown): what they care about, recurring
  images, how they talk, contradictions. This keeps future songs in character.
- voice: one line describing the lead vocal the music model should hear on every song (range,
  timbre, delivery, language/accent). "instrumental" if the act has no vocals. Never change it
  between songs.
- vocal_tags: the same lead vocal as short comma-separated tags for the music model's caption,
  starting with who sings (e.g. "female vocals, breathy soprano, intimate"); "instrumental"
  for an act without vocals.
- genres: 2 to 5 free-text genre or scene labels as a music journalist would use them.
- members: people in the act with roles (invented). A solo act has one member.
- visual: how the artist looks and presents, for press photos and covers: the visible identity
  a photographer would need (apparent age, gender presentation, heritage as the bio has it,
  hair, build, number of people), their contemporary stage and shoot styling named concretely
  (garments, colours, accessories), and the mood of their imagery. Style them as the working
  artists they are today; describe traditional or festive dress only when it is genuinely the
  act's chosen look, and say so explicitly.
- languages: the language(s) they sing in, as ISO 639-1 codes, chosen from the act's identity.
